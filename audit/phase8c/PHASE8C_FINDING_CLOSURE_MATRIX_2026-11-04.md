## Findings accounting and boundaries

- **Baseline original findings:** 13 (`P8C-001`…`P8C-013`), **11 P1 and 2 P2**. All **13** have direct corrected-source retest evidence above. **CPU hot loop** is an additional P1 corrective record and also closed by its own resource test. All are `IMPLEMENTED — DEVICE VERIFICATION PENDING` for Phase 9, not a Phase 8C hardware PASS.

This final matrix supersedes their **current closure statuses**, not the original failure evidence.
