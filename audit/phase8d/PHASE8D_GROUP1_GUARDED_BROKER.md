## Cleanup is distinct from a seal acknowledgement

Guarded release always attempts authority cleanup even if its initial guard check throws or times out. It then requires confirmed guard clearance and the expected authority epoch before acknowledging success. Any uncertain pre-check, poisoned post-check or cleanup failure refuses the signed success reply. A timed-out check does not count as permission, and cleanup does not clear the independent guard. Ordinary/runtime writer enter/start/cancel/exit do not depend on guard availability; urgent outage work still requires a durable independent quarantine before its source effect.
